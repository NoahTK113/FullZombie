ZOMBIE GAME RULES
=================

Version:     002
Date:        2026-09-30
Supersedes:  001
Status:      draft. Nothing below is approved until it appears in a released version.

This file is authoritative and immutable. It is never edited in place. To change
any statement, a complete new version is written, checked for internal
consistency as a whole, approved line by line, and released as the next numbered
file. The newest file in this folder is the current truth.

Categories are for reading only. A statement applies everywhere regardless of
the category it is filed under.

Abbreviations: KC = kill currency. RM = raw materials.

GENERAL
=======

- Exploration requires capacity.

- Capacity requires exploration.

- Combat requires capacity.

- Capacity requires combat.

- The player starts with some capacity for combat and for exploration.

- Most players finish.

- No sub-part crafting: every item is usable or placeable.

- No upkeep on food, water, etc.

- No passive upkeep on machines or buildings.

- No passive upkeep on anything that is not a direct consequence of player
  action.

- Examples of non-passive upkeep are restocking ammunition and rebuilding
  structures that zombies damage during combat.

- No degradation of weapons or other useful items.

- Quickly regenerating health; no health packs or similar.


COMBAT
======

- Difficulty is fixed to the round, not to time or location.

- Difficulty comes from horde size and speed, not from time to kill.

- Zombie health is low relative to the player's weapons. No bullet sponges.

- Combat is active, not passive: no hiding behind walls.

- No automated combat.

- Zombie health scales with round, tuned to estimated player weapon strength.


ROUND PROGRESSION AND ANCHOR
============================

- One Anchor zombie exists per round, tied to the round number.

- Killing the Anchor starts the next round.

- The Anchor and all zombies always move toward the player.

- Rounds can overlap if the Anchor is killed before its horde is completely
  destroyed.

- The Anchor or zombies can be enclosed.

- An enclosed Anchor or zombie breaks only what lies between it and the player.

- The Anchor and all zombies always break all barriers in their path to the
  player, whether or not the player is present.

- Zombies only damage structures that completely prevent access to the player.


WORLD AND EXPLORATION
=====================

- Open world.

- Atmosphere.

- No hard-gated locations.

- Location access is gated by navigation difficulty and hazards.

- Self-sufficient institutional campus.

- The campus has all necessary infrastructure: living, farming, recreation,
  infirmary, power, food, maintenance.

- Large central quarantined building or facility.

- The endgame is at the center of the facility.

- Progression is inward: the facility is layered like an onion.

- Three to four distinct layers of location progression, with a distinct
  atmosphere in each.

- Each exploration layer contains new and more powerful machines.


PROGRESSION TIERS
=================

- Progression tiers are defined by location.

- Each progression tier is characterized by the machines and raw materials
  available in its locations.

- Tier one has a weapons machine.

- Tier one has a building machine.


ECONOMY
=======

- There are two main currency types: kill currency (KC) and raw materials (RM).

- Kill currency is earned only by damaging or killing zombies.

- Kill currency is a quantity tied to the living character, not an inventory
  item.

- Raw materials are found in the world and picked up.

- Raw materials are carried or stored.

- Carried raw materials and items are lost upon death.


MACHINES
========

- All usable items come from machines.

- A machine prints copies of its own type only.

- Items and machine copies cost KC and RM.

- Copies of machines are carried and placed, and can be lost.

- Using machines costs kill currency and raw materials.

- All items and raw materials are stored in the same inventory or storage, in
  the same slots.

- Machines are the primary findables.

- Each machine type exists as one or more immovable originals somewhere in the
  world.

- Machines are self-replicating, at a cost of KC and RM.

- Original machines cannot be lost. Copies made from originals can be carried
  and placed.

- Placed machines can be picked up again and moved.

- The weapons machine allows players to create weapons from raw materials and
  kill currency.


WEAPONS
=======

- The tier one weapons machine contains all base weapons.

- Base weapons contain 10 to 20 firearms.

- Base weapons contain 1 to 2 melee weapons.

- Base weapon firearms span all basic firearm types.

- Basic firearm types are: semi-automatic handgun, semi-automatic rifle,
  automatic submachine gun, automatic assault rifle, automatic large machine
  gun, pump shotgun, break action shotgun, sniper rifle, bolt action rifle.

- Base weapons also include 1 to 2 types of explosives, including hand grenades
  and maybe one other.

- Base weapons also include 1 to 2 types of special weapons, to be determined.


DEATH
=====

- Death resets the round to the starting round and difficulty, not the world.

- The character and their abilities are lost on death.

- The base, machines, stored RM and items persist through death.

- Lost means gone forever: nothing is recoverable from the place of death.

- Kill currency is lost on death.
